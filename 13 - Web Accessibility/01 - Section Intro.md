# Section Intro

We're going to take a little break from the CSS and look at web accessibility. Something that I think is missing from a lot of web dev courses. It's easy to forget that there are people that view our websites with certain disabilities that prevent them from seeing or interacting with content the way most users do. Ensuring our websites are accessible means making them usable for everyone, regardless of their abilities. In this section, we'll learn about ways to make our sites more accessible.
