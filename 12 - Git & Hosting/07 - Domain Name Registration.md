# Domain Name Registration

We have a live website with a Netlify domain. Now we want to register a custom domain name for our site. This will make it easier for people to find our site and it will look more professional.

There are many domain registrars that you can use. I prefer to use Namecheap. They have good prices and good customer service. They are not sponsoring this course nor is anyone else. These are just services that I use and like.

Sign up at [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/). Once you have an account, you can search for a domain name that you want. I have registered the domain `luminacreative.net` for this course.

Once you have a domain registered, you need to point it to your Netlify site.

You can follow the instructions at https://dev.to/easybuoy/setting-up-domain-with-namecheap-netlify-1a4d
